3D MathGrid

Instructions

Moving around the 3D view

  • Rotate (orbit) Touch: drag with one finger Mouse: left-drag
  • Zoom in / out (dolly) Touch: pinch two fingers together or apart Mouse: scroll wheel
  • Slide sideways / up / down (pan) Touch: drag with two fingers Mouse: right-drag, or Shift + left-drag Keyboard: arrow keys

Decimals

Switch Decimals on (in Place Value or in Add & Subtract) to read the pieces as fractions of one whole cube of 1000 pieces. The whole cube is 1, a flat of 100 is 0.1, a row of ten is 0.01 and a single piece is 0.001. With Decimals on, each whole is drawn as one solid block (a sphere in sphere mode) (a row of ten blocks is 10, a flat of a hundred blocks is 100) in the whole-number colours, while the decimal pieces use cool colours. In Add & Subtract, numbers run from 0.000 to 999.999: type the whole part (hundreds, tens, ones) to the left of the point and the decimals to the right.

Building the structure

Drag the sliders to build. Scale: Units → Tens → Hundreds → Thousands → 10K → 100K → Million.

Add & Subtract

Type a number into the A row and the B row (hundreds, tens, units - or, with Decimals on, whole numbers and decimals), tap + / − to choose add or subtract, then tap = to start. Pile A is the number you start with and pile B is the amount being added or taken away.

Tap > to move one place at a time, starting with the units, or >> to play every step. The answer fills in under the line as each place finishes. Tap × to change the numbers.

When you subtract more than pile A has, we borrow a ten from the next place. If there is nothing left to borrow, the missing amount shows as faint, translucent shapes in pile A and the answer becomes negative.

The Quiz button next to the tab asks you to guess the answer before the demo proves it.

Color Saturation

0 = grayscale, 100 = normal, 200 = vivid.
